About Paimpaal

Our story, our farm, and the quality we stand behind.

Our Story

Paimpaal is a family-run farm nestled in the hills of India. What started as a passion for sustainable farming has grown into a thriving homestead producing fresh dairy, seasonal produce, tropical fruits, and aromatic spices.

We believe in doing things the right way — no shortcuts, no chemicals, just honest farming. Every product that leaves our farm is something we'd proudly serve at our own table.

Beyond the farm, we opened our doors to guests who want to experience the beauty of rural life. Our hilltop farmstay offers a peaceful escape with views of the Western Ghats that you have to see to believe.

Meet Mujika

The heart (and stomach) of the farm. Mujika and her friends provide the fresh milk that goes into our dairy products — from creamy yogurt to golden ghee.

They graze freely on the farm's lush hilltop pastures, and in return, they give us the best milk you'll ever taste.
